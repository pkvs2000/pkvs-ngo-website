ABOUT PKVS

Committed to community development.

Prabhat Kendra Vikas Sanstha is a community-focused organization working across key areas that can improve everyday life and create sustainable opportunities.

Our work is centered on education, agriculture, health and social help, with a vision of inclusive development and stronger communities.

WHAT WE DO

Areas of our work

Focused initiatives designed around community needs.

📚

Education

Supporting learning, awareness and opportunities that help individuals and communities move forward.

🌾

Agriculture

Encouraging agricultural development and stronger livelihood opportunities for rural communities.

🩺

Health

Promoting health awareness and community support for a healthier and more informed society.

🤝

Social Help

Standing with people and communities through social support, assistance and welfare-oriented activities.

OUR VISION

Building stronger communities, one step at a time.

We believe lasting development comes from opportunity, awareness, participation and support. PKVS aims to contribute to a more educated, healthy and self-reliant society.
